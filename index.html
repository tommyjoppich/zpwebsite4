About

A fresh approach on trading through collaborative problem solving.

2019

Humble since our beginnings.

ZeroPlus Derivatives was founded in 2019 after finding a unique opportunity in options and building a strategy around it. It has since blossomed into several strategies across a wide range of different products.

In the ever-changing world of financial markets, there's little room for hierarchy. Everyone has a voice. We employ new and experienced traders, software developers, and quantitative analysts to work alongside us.

Today

Every great opportunity starts at zero.

We aim to remove emotion from the equation. Our data-driven approach eliminates risk as we add liquidity and provide transparency — trading across all 16 US options exchanges on our own in-house software, both programmatically and manually.
